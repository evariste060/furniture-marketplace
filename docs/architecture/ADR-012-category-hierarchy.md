# ADR-012: Category hierarchy (main category and subcategory)

Status: **Accepted** by the project owner on 2026-11-16. Trello card: not yet given (none is invented here). Scope: categories, brands and product assignment in the backend, the admin and the storefront.

## Context

The `categories` table has always allowed nesting (`parent_id`, ADR-002), but the storefront and the product editor were built for one level: the public category filter matched one category exactly, so a main category's page would have lost its products once they moved into subcategories, and the product editor offered one flat list. The PRD leaves the final taxonomy to the business owner. Brands are their own table, and the brand filter already gives "Samsung within Phones".

## Decisions

1. **Product types are subcategories; brands stay brands** (option C of the inspection report). "Phones & Tablets › Smartphones" with brand Samsung, not a "Samsung" subcategory. Which brands a category's filters offer comes from its products (the filter counts already work that way). The only brand-to-category link is the menu list in decision 8.
2. **Two levels**: main category, then subcategory. The staff API refuses a parent that is not a main category, and a parent for a category that has subcategories (422 on `parent_id`). Queries are recursive, so a deeper tree created before the cap keeps working.
3. **A product stores one category.** It may be a main category or one of its subcategories (a subcategory is optional). The main category is that category's root, so the two can never disagree, and no `main_category_id` column is added.
4. **No new assignment to a hidden category or brand**, in the staff API and in the catalogue import. A category is hidden when it or its main category is inactive. A product already there keeps its category and brand, and other edits to it still work. This replaces the earlier rule that inactive categories and brands were accepted.
5. **Hidden main category, hidden subtree, everywhere**: the public tree already hid it; now the product list, search, facets, product page, cart and checkout sellability and the staff stock view use the same rule (`app/repositories/category_tree.py`).
6. **A main category's listing includes its subcategories' products** (`?category=` takes the subtree), and so do its filter counts, the menu's best sellers, the home spotlights and related products. On brand and search pages the Category filter group is counted per main category.
7. **Reordering**: `PUT /staff/categories/order` renumbers the categories under one parent 1, 2, 3, ... in one transaction with one audit record (`catalog.category_reorder`). The admin's arrows use it; the sort-order field left the category dialog, and new or moved categories go last.
8. **Menu brand lists** (approved 2026-11-16, amending decision 1): staff choose, per main category, the brands the storefront's "All Categories" menu lists, in their order, even before a brand has products there. Table `category_menu_brands` (category_id, brand_id, position; primary key on the pair, position unique per category; both foreign keys ON DELETE CASCADE, because the list is presentation, not history). `PUT /staff/categories/{id}/menu-brands` replaces the list in one transaction with one audit record (`catalog.category_menu_brands`, old and new lists); only a main category has one, every brand must exist, and a brand not already listed must be active. The public tree gives each category its active listed brands (`menu_brands`); a hidden brand stays in the staff list but leaves the storefront. A main category without a list shows the brands of its products. The initial lists (Phones & Tablets: Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, TECNO, Infinix, Huawei, Google, OPPO, Vivo, Motorola; Laptops & Computers: Apple, HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, Microsoft, MSI, Samsung, Huawei; TVs & Audio: Samsung, LG, Hisense, Sony, TCL, Philips, Panasonic, JBL, Bose, Xiaomi) and the brand names TECNO and OPPO (slugs unchanged; Google keeps its name, Pixel being its phone line) come from the owner on 2026-11-16 and are applied by `scripts/apply_category_taxonomy.py`.
9. **Brand categories** (approved 2026-11-17, amending decision 1): a brand belongs to one or more **main** categories, chosen when it is added (required; `category_ids` on `POST /staff/brands`, at least one, each once, at most 50) and changeable later (`PATCH` replaces them; at least one when sent). Table `brand_categories` (brand_id, category_id; primary key on the pair, index on category_id, both foreign keys ON DELETE CASCADE). A subcategory or an unknown category is refused (422 on `category_ids`); the change is in the brand’s audit record. When a main category is moved under another, its brands move to that new main category (recorded in the category’s audit as `brands_moved_to_parent`), so no brand loses its category; deleting a category removes its links. The links describe brands for staff: each main category has a Brands tab, the Brands list a Categories column and filter, and the product editor and the store menu picker offer the category’s brands first. They never restrict products (a product may still have any brand). A brand in a main category’s store menu belongs to that category (owner, 2026-11-17): saving the menu links any listed brand not linked yet (recorded as `brands_linked` in the menu audit), the migration linked the brands already in menus, and a category cannot be taken from a brand while its store menu lists it (422 on `category_ids`; take it off the menu first). Taking a brand off a menu keeps the link. A category’s brands may be more than its menu shows, and the menu still decides what the storefront lists (decision 8: Home Appliances keeps listing its subcategories). The storefront does not use the links. Other existing brands are given the main categories of their products by `scripts/backfill_brand_categories.py` (dry run first; it only adds); brands with neither products nor a menu stay unlinked, flagged in the admin until staff choose. A brand added before 2026-11-17 may have none until edited, and editing it asks for one.
10. **Store category bar** (owner, 2026-11-17): the blue bar shows All Categories, then each main category as text (no icons) with a hover dropdown of its own subcategories (a category without subcategories is a plain link; clicking the name opens the category page; a caret button opens the list for keyboard and touch), then Services and, last, Deals (owner, 2026-11-18: a red block with white text, --err with --err-tx on hover, so it stands apart from the categories; red text on the blue bar would be unreadable). The Brands dropdown and Track Order are no longer on the bar; Track Order is in the actions row above, before My Account. The main categories were renamed to short names (Laptops, Phones, TVs, Home Appliances, Accessories, Cameras; slugs and URLs unchanged), so those names show everywhere. The All Categories mega menu and the mobile menu are unchanged.
11. **Subcategories on the category page** (owner, 2026-11-17): the page no longer shows subcategory links above the listing. The filters (desktop panel and phone sheet) start with a Subcategory group: “All <main category>” and each of its subcategories, drawn as radio options. Choosing one opens that (sub)category’s page at once, keeping the other filters and the sort; the one being browsed is selected. A main category without subcategories has no such group.
12. **Trusted Brands on the home page** (owner, 2026-11-17): just above the footer, a centred "Trusted Brands" heading over a row of the brands' logos on white cards (rounded, light border and shadow) moving from right to left in an endless loop, and under it the number of brands ("40+ brands available": the exact number below 20, otherwise rounded down to tens), linking to /brands. Both come from the same cached `GET /catalog/brands` (active brands). Only brands with an uploaded logo are shown; all are counted. Each card links to the brand's page; the motion pauses on hover or focus and stops with reduced motion (the row then scrolls). Without any logo the section is left out.

## Design changes (approved 2026-11-16)

- **Product editor**: Main category, then Subcategory (only the chosen main category's subcategories; reset when the main category changes; "None" keeps the product in the main category), then Brand and Model. Hidden categories and brands are not offered, except a product's current one, marked "(hidden)". `ProductEditor.dc.html` is updated.
- **Category pages**: a row of subcategory links under the header ("All <main category>" first, the page shown marked), on the main category's page and on each subcategory's page. `Category.dc.html` and `MobileCategory.dc.html` are updated.
- **Admin Categories & brands page** (redesigned 2026-11-16 at the owner’s request, replacing the side-by-side tree and brand panels): two tabs, `/admin/categories` and `/admin/brands` (the sidebar item covers both). Categories: the main categories on the left with their totals (products in total, subcategories) and a search that also finds subcategories, shown under their main category; the chosen one (kept in the URL as `?category=<id>&tab=`) opens on the right with its name, slug and totals, the “Shown on the store” switch, move up and down, and Edit, and three tabs: **Subcategories** (a table with products linking to the product list, the shown switch, move up and down, Edit, and “Add subcategory”), **Store menu** (what “All Categories” lists beside it under decision 8 — its brand list in order, otherwise its subcategories, otherwise the brands of its products — with “Edit brand list” or “Choose brands”) and **Details** (description, image, spotlight product, “Edit details”). Brands: one table with search and a status filter, each brand’s logo, slug, products (linking to the product list), the main categories whose store menu lists it (linking to that menu) and the shown switch.
- **Admin look and quick add** (owner request, 2026-11-16): the page shows four totals (main categories, subcategories, brands, hidden); each category and brand shows a green “Shown” or grey “Hidden” state; the chosen category has a summary row (products, subcategories, what the store menu shows, visibility and position) and “View on store”. Subcategories are added without a dialog: under the table, type a name and press Enter (the box stays ready for the next), or paste a list, one per line, to add several in order after the existing ones; a name already under that main category is skipped and a refused one (e.g. its web address taken) stays in the box with the reason. “More options” opens the full dialog with the name filled in. The “Add main category” dialog takes its subcategories too, one per line. Each is an ordinary `POST /staff/categories` (slug from the name), so authorization, validation and audit are unchanged.
- **Admin category dialog** (a pop-up, as chosen by the owner): editing a category has tabs Basics (name, parent, slug, description, shown, delete hints), Image & spotlight, and, for a main category, Store menu (“Brands in the store menu”: the chosen brands in order, with move up, move down and remove, and “Add a brand…” offering the active brands not listed yet, at most 30). A refused save opens the tab with the message. Adding a category shows Basics only. `Categories.dc.html` predates this layout, so it is described here.
- **Product page breadcrumb**: Home / main category / subcategory / brand / product.
- **"All Categories" menu, in three steps** (approved 2026-11-16, revised the same day): main categories on the left; hovering (or focusing, or a first tap on) one shows the middle column: its menu brand list when it has one (decision 8: Phones & Tablets, Laptops & Computers, TVs & Audio), otherwise its subcategories (Home Appliances, Accessories, Cameras & Photography, so customers who do not know the brands find the product type), and for a category with neither, the brands that have public products in it. Hovering a subcategory shows its best sellers and clicking it opens `/search?category=<subcategory>`. Staff switch a category between brands and subcategories by adding or clearing its brand list, with product counts where there are any and "All <main category>" first; a listed brand without products there says so and loads nothing; hovering a brand shows that brand's best sellers in the category (Apple in Phones & Tablets: the iPhones). The products are the brand's, not a product line's: the catalogue has no "iPhone" or "MacBook" field, so Apple stands for both. A category without brands shows only its own best sellers. The categories' brands and best sellers come with the page (the header reads them on the server, cached); a brand's products are loaded through the site's API proxy the first time it is shown and kept for five minutes. There is no desktop design file for this menu, so none is updated.
- **Menu clicks open the category page** (approved 2026-11-16 as the search page; changed by the owner on 2026-11-17): a main category, a subcategory or "View all" in All Categories opens `/category/<slug>`, the same page as its link on the menu bar; a brand opens `/category/<slug>?brand=<brand>` (the category page with that brand ticked); a product its product page. The admin’s “View on store” opens the category page too. The search page still accepts `?category=` without text (links people kept): the heading is the category's name, the chips are "All <main category>" and its subcategories, and "Clear all" keeps the category. An unknown slug without text shows the search prompt. Subcategories stay on the category and search pages; the category pages (`/category/<slug>`) and the nav row's category links are unchanged.

## Consequences

- The `?category=` filter of `GET /catalog/products` and `GET /catalog/facets` now returns a subtree; the storefront is its only consumer. With no subcategories (the data before this change) every response is the same as before.
- The hierarchy itself needed no migration (the schema already had `parent_id` and the `(parent_id, is_active)` index). The menu brand lists add one table, `category_menu_brands` (migration `a7d2e9c4b1f6`); the runtime role gets SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE on it (`scripts/provision_runtime_role.py`).
- The initial taxonomy is data, not code: it is applied by a re-runnable script after the owner approves the category and product mapping, and administrators change it afterwards in the admin.
- Kinyarwanda strings added for this are drafts in `docs/frontend/rw-review*.md`, like the others.
