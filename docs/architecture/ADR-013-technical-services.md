# ADR-013: Technical services and service requests

Status: **Accepted** by the project owner on 2026-11-17 (scope change SC-02). Trello card: none (owner, 2026-11-17). Scope: a Services module in the backend, the storefront and the admin, kept apart from the product catalogue.

## Context

3 Sixty Technologies also sells technical work: CCTV, gate automation, access control, networking, electronics repair and training. These are not products: they have no price, stock or cart, and the business quotes each job after talking to the client. The owner asked for service pages in the main navigation and a "Request a service" form whose submissions staff follow up, like the business quote requests (module 19).

## Decisions

1. **Own tables, not catalogue categories.** `service_categories` (one service: slug, texts, icon, one image, order, shown or hidden), `service_items` (its "What we offer" bullet points, in order; CASCADE with the service, at most 30) and `service_requests` (the leads). The catalogue's `categories` table, its filters and its menus are unchanged.
2. **Two languages in columns.** Staff write each text in English (required) and Kinyarwanda (optional): `name_en/_rw`, `short_description_en/_rw`, `intro_en/_rw`, `title_en/_rw`. The public API takes `?locale=en|rw` and answers the Kinyarwanda text where one is written, otherwise the English one. The seed leaves Kinyarwanda empty until reviewed translations are entered in the admin.
3. **Icons from a fixed list** (`cctv`, `gate`, `fingerprint`, `network`, `wrench`, `graduation-cap`, `shield`, `cpu`), checked by the database and mapped to Lucide icons by the frontend. No uploaded icons.
4. **Images in the media registry** (ADR-009) with owner `services`, exactly like category images: one public source and its private original, replaced or removed by staff; the cleanup worker deletes retired files. Until a photo is uploaded the storefront shows a placeholder (the brand's light tint with the service's icon); no placeholder files are stored.
5. **Requests (leads).** `POST /services/{slug}/requests` takes the full name, a **Rwandan mobile** number (stored canonical), the client type (`home`, `business`, `institution`), and optionally an email, a location or district (free text, at most 120 characters), a message (at most 2,000) and a preferred date (today to one year ahead, Kigali time), with the reply language. Each request gets a reference `SR-YYMMDD-XXXXXX` (Kigali date, the quote alphabet without 0/O/1/I).
6. **Spam controls.** A hidden `website` field (honeypot): a request that fills it is answered like a stored one, but nothing is stored, counted or emailed. Every other attempt counts against a fixed-window limit per phone number (its keyed pseudonym, never the number) and per client network: 3 and 10 an hour by default (`SERVICE_REQUEST_*`). Beyond it, 429 `rate_limited` with `Retry-After`; an unreachable limit store refuses with 503 `service_request_unavailable`.
7. **Acknowledgement.** A request with an email gets the `service_received` email through the notification outbox (event `service_received`, subject type `service`), with a link to the service in the client's language. There is no SMS and no staff email; staff see the count of new requests in the admin sidebar and the bell.
8. **Statuses**: `new`, `contacted`, `scheduled`, `completed`, `cancelled`. Staff may move a request to any status; `status_changed_at` is set when it leaves `new` and cleared when it goes back. A request may be assigned to one active staff member who can manage services (null unassigns), and has one `internal_notes` text (at most 4,000 characters) that only staff see. The admin inbox `/admin/service-requests` (layout owner, 2026-11-18) shows one card per status with its count (the status filter), filters by service, assignee (`assigned_to`) and the day sent, lists each request with quick WhatsApp, and opens it in a drawer (with Call, WhatsApp and Email) with the path New → Contacted → Scheduled → Completed, a one-click next step, cancel and reopen, the follow-up fields and the request's activity (received, `status_changed_at`, `updated_at`); it uses only the existing staff endpoints.
9. **Permissions**: `services:read` (administrator, sales, management) lists services and requests; `services:manage` (administrator, sales) changes services, items and images and follows requests up. No new role. Every change is audited (`services.service_create/_update/_delete/_reorder/_image_upload/_image_delete`, `services.item_create/_update/_delete/_reorder`, `services.request_update`); a change that changes nothing writes nothing. Long texts (intros, notes) are recorded as changed, not copied into the audit trail.
10. **Delete rule.** A service with requests cannot be deleted (409 `service_has_requests`; `RESTRICT` in the database): staff hide it instead. A hidden service's page and request form answer 404 and it leaves the navigation, the footer and the "Other services" cards.
11. **Order.** `PUT /staff/services/order` renumbers every service 1, 2, 3, ... (all of them, each once; 409 `service_order_mismatch` otherwise) and `PUT /staff/services/{id}/items/order` does the same for a service's items. The admin's up/down arrows use them.
12. **Storefront.** `/services` (owner, 2026-11-17: a split hero with the title, intro, "Explore services" and "Talk to our team" on the left and a collage of the services' own photos, or their icon placeholders, on the right, since no separate hero image is stored; the service cards four to a desktop row, two on tablets and large phones, one on small phones; a promotional card "Tell us what you need" two grid places wide (a full row on tablets), white like the service cards, closing the 2nd row (after the 6th service, or right after the last when there are fewer), with WhatsApp, Call and Email (owner, 2026-11-18: three buttons of the same width, the icon over the label; each left out while unset; `/contact` when none is set; Email opens a `mailto:` to the store's email with a subject line); it replaces the page's former closing "Talk to our team" band, which the hero's "Talk to our team" now scrolls to). Service cards (owner, 2026-11-17; one component on the home page, `/services` and "Other services", both grids four to a desktop row) carry Details (the service page) and Book Now (its request wizard) like the product cards' two actions, and flip in 3D: the front shows the photo, name and card text, the back "What's included" with the service's own items from `GET /services` (six, then "+ n more on the details page"; a note when it has none) and the same two actions. A mouse flips a card on hover; touch and keyboard users use its "See what's included" and "Back to overview" buttons (Esc also turns it back); the hidden face is inert. Services have no price, so none is shown and `/services/[slug]` (breadcrumb, hero with "Request this service", "What we offer", "Who it's for" (homes, offices, shops, institutions; static text), a "Request this service" panel beside Call and WhatsApp, and the other services). Both "Request this service" buttons (and the mega menu's) open `/services/[slug]/request` (owner, 2026-11-17), the request as a step-by-step wizard: 1 the service (its text and items from `GET /services/{slug}`), 2 an optional preferred date (today to a year ahead; there are no time slots, the team calls to agree the time), 3 the customer's details, 4 a review with Edit per section, 5 the confirmation with the reference and status "Received" (the request's status `new`). It uses the same fields, validation and `POST /services/{slug}/requests` the single form did; Continue checks only the current step, Back keeps what was typed, and a server 422 returns to the step with the mistake. The page is not indexed. Each page has its own title, description and Open Graph image (the service's photo when there is one). The category bar shows "Services" after the categories and before Deals (Deals moved last on 2026-11-18), as text (no icon, like the categories), pinned (never under "More"), with a dropdown of the services and their icons; the footer has a Services column and the mobile menu a Services row.
14. **Home page and All Categories** (owner, 2026-11-17, replacing "no home page strip"): the home page shows, in this order, the hero, Featured Products, **Our Services** (every active service as its card, "All services" beside the heading; since 2026-11-18 with the same "Tell us what you need" card in the same place as on `/services`, from one shared `ServiceGrid`), the category spotlights, Best Selling Products, New Arrivals and the business band; How Shopping Works is removed. The hero's rotating showcase treats Services like a main category: one more place on the orbit after the categories, with one image standing for all services (the brand's light tint with the wrench, as on the menu's Services entry; no photo is stored for it). In the spotlight it shows "Technical Services", "Explore services" and "View all (n)", and the first four services come in on the right as tiles (photo or icon placeholder and name, linking to the service's page; "View all (n)" leads to the rest), the way a category's next four best sellers do. The "All Categories" mega menu ends with **Services**, treated as a main category: hovering it lists the services as its subcategories, with every service's card text on the right; hovering a service shows its text, what it includes and "Request this service". All of it reads the same cached `GET /services` as the nav; no service data is stored or written in the frontend.
13. **Seed.** `scripts/seed_services.py` (development only, dry run first) creates the six services from the brief and their items through the application service, as the seed administrator; a service whose slug exists is left as staff keep it. Services 4 (IT infrastructure) and 6 (technical training) and every intro are drafts for the owner to confirm.

## Consequences

- Three new tables and three widened check constraints (media owner, notification event and subject) in migration `e5a1c7d9b3f2`; the runtime role gets SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE on services and items, and SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE on requests (the application never deletes a request).
- The storefront reads services with the cached catalogue reads (about a minute), so a change in the admin reaches the store within that time.
- Requests hold personal data (name, phone, optional email and location). They are visible only to staff with `services:read`; nothing deletes them yet, as with quote requests.
