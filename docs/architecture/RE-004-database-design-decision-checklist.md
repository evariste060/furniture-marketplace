# RE-004: Database Design Decision Checklist

Use this checklist to resolve design questions for ADR-002 without treating requirements as approvals. **Confirmed** means stated in the cited source document or RE-004 task brief; **proposed** means an engineering default for review; **unresolved** means the business or technical decision/evidence is still needed. ADR-002 remains **Proposed** until its required approvals are recorded.

| Decision/question | Why it matters | Verified source requirement or QA test | Evidence-based proposed default, if any | Responsible owner (only if documented) | Status | Approval/evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep physical stock movements separate from temporary reservations? | Prevents reservation creation/release from being misreported as goods received or dispatched and supports reconciliation. | Technical Plan §6: safe inventory updates and agreed reservation policy; PR-09; QA TC-13, TC-14. | Separate append-only physical `StockMovement` from `StockReservation`; reservation-only state changes create no movement. | Engineering lead (implementation responsibility per Technical Plan RACI); policy owner unassigned. | Requirement confirmed; proposed design. | Approve movement types, opening-stock treatment, and reconciliation rule. |
| Which quantities reduce availability? | An inconsistent balance can oversell or hide stock. | PR-09; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-13. | `available = on_hand - active_reservations - committed_reservations - unavailable_stock`; subtract unavailable stock only if included in on-hand, never twice. | Engineering lead; business policy owner unassigned. | Proposed; approval needed. | Confirm whether on-hand includes damaged/quarantined stock and approve the balance invariant. |
| When do reservations start, expire, and permit retry? | Determines checkout availability, abandoned-order handling, and payment timeouts. | PR-05, PR-09; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-06, TC-07, TC-13, TC-14. | Atomically reserve all order lines at the approved order-acceptance point; expiry/retry mechanism must be idempotent. No source specifies start point, TTL, or renewal. | Business owner and Technical Product Lead/engineering lead are relevant per PRD/RACI; exact policy owner unassigned. | Requirement confirmed; policy unresolved. | Approve trigger, TTL, renewal rule, retry window, and expiry worker ownership. |
| How do failure, cancellation, and late payment affect holds? | A released hold may be unavailable when a delayed success arrives; paid cancellations also need refund coordination. | PR-08, PR-09; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-11, TC-14; PRD §7 assigns cancellation/refund policy. | Conditional once-only release; late success must recheck stock and follow an approved refund/manual-resolution path if stock cannot be safely re-reserved. Do not silently confirm or oversell. | Business + finance + technical lead for payment decisions; Business + finance/legal adviser for cancellation policy. Individual approvers unassigned. | Requirement confirmed; outcome policy unresolved. | Approve release timing, terminal states, late-success remedy, cancellation authority, and refund/stock reconciliation. |
| At what event does physical stock leave inventory, and how are returns handled? | Avoids double counting committed reservations and shipped goods; return stock may not be sellable. | PR-07, PR-09; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-14 and UAT operations flow; PRD §7. | At dispatch, atomically decrement on-hand, append one outbound movement, and mark the matching committed reservation fulfilled. Return stock changes sellable on-hand only after approved inspection/receipt. | Operations owns delivery-zone/partner decisions in PRD; cancellation/return policy owner is Business + finance/legal adviser. Inventory policy approver unassigned. | Proposed; operational confirmation needed. | Confirm dispatch/handoff event, partial dispatch, return inspection, quarantine/write-off, and restocking movements. |
| What concurrency rule protects multi-line checkout? | Prevents simultaneous checkouts from consuming the same available stock. | Technical Plan §6; PR-09; QA TC-13, TC-14. | One database transaction reserves all lines or none, using row locks or conditional atomic updates in stable variant order; release/fulfil via conditional state transition. | Engineering lead responsible for architecture/security/deployment per Technical Plan RACI. | Requirement confirmed; mechanism proposed. | Approve implementation mechanism and provide concurrency tests for zero stock, competing orders, rollback, expiry/cancel races, and dispatch. |
| Are guest and registered-account checkout both supported? | Both are stated MVP checkout paths. | PR-05 explicitly says guest and account checkout; QA TC-06 (guest) and TC-07 (account). | Support both. A guest order may have no registered-user FK but must retain required order, contact, and delivery information. | Unassigned in source. | Confirmed requirement. | No approval to make guest checkout optional; decide only the implementation details in the rows below. |
| How are guest carts/session ownership and account conversion handled? | Affects cart persistence, uniqueness, expiry, and whether carts can merge after sign-in. | PR-05; QA TC-05, TC-06. | Keep cart ownership independent of price/stock authority; no source mandates a guest token or merge behavior. | Unassigned in source. | Unresolved implementation choice. | Decide guest cart/session identity, expiry, merge behavior, and protection of cart access. |
| How can a guest securely retrieve an order, and what data is retained? | Guest orders need usable status/contact flows without exposing another customer's order or retaining excess personal data. | PR-05, PR-07, PR-15; QA TC-06, TC-20; PRD §7 privacy policy owner. | Preserve the order and delivery/contact snapshots even without a user FK; do not infer an authentication/retrieval mechanism. | Business + finance/legal adviser owns privacy policy per PRD; technical/security owner unassigned. | Checkout requirement confirmed; retrieval/retention unresolved. | Approve secure retrieval approach, snapshot fields, access lifetime, retention/deletion/anonymization rules, and privacy review. |
| What catalogue relationships and taxonomy are required? | Determines category hierarchy, product assignment, and category/brand browse queries. | PRD §§3/7; PR-02, PR-03; QA TC-02, TC-03. | `Category` and `Brand` referenced by `Product`; keep generic relationships separate from taxonomy seed data. | Business owner owns final taxonomy per PRD; Merchandising/business supplies catalogue input. | Capability confirmed; taxonomy unresolved. | Obtain RE-002's real approval/evidence for final categories, brand data, and any schema-affecting taxonomy rules. Do not block generic relationship design on catalogue seed data. |
| How are SKU-bearing variants and attributes represented? | Variant-specific price/stock and correct product display/order history depend on a stable sellable unit. | PRD §3; PR-03, PR-10; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-04, TC-15 and UAT laptop scenario. | Parent product plus SKU-bearing variant for products with colour/storage/RAM differences; exact attribute structure and single-SKU-product handling remain open. | Merchandising/business supplies catalogue input; design approver unassigned. | Requirement confirmed; schema choice proposed. | Approve required fields, attribute validation, SKU uniqueness scope, product-without-variant policy, specification/image representation, and catalogue import mapping. |
| Which order and address details must be snapshotted? | Historical orders must remain explainable after catalogue/address changes and support guest checkout. | RE-004 acceptance criterion 4 (task brief); PR-05, PR-06, PR-07; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-06, TC-07, TC-18, TC-24. | Snapshot purchased SKU, product/variant identity and selected attributes, quantity, charged price/discount/tax, delivery fee/zone, contact/address, currency, and relevant displayed detail. | Technical Product Lead accountable for architecture; business owner approves commercial/customer-facing terms. | RE-004 snapshot criterion confirmed; exact snapshot set unresolved. | Approve snapshot fields, treatment of specifications/warranty/images, amendment policy, and personal-data retention. |
| What order states and transitions are allowed? | Explicit valid transitions are required for staff workflow, payment coordination, cancellation, and dispatch. | PR-07; Technical Plan §6; QA UAT operations flow and TC-14. | Keep fulfilment state separate from payment state; preserve PRD terms received, confirmed, processing, dispatched, delivered, cancelled; validate transitions and record actor/time/reason. | Business owner for cancellation policy (PRD §7); Operations for fulfilment workflow; engineering lead for enforcement. | Required vocabulary confirmed; transition policy unresolved. | Approve transition graph, cancellation cut-off/authority, partial fulfilment, returns, and state history. |
| How are multiple payment attempts/events and callbacks reconciled? | Retries and delayed/duplicate/out-of-order callbacks must not double-settle an order or regress a successful payment. | PR-08; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-08, TC-09, TC-10, TC-11, TC-12, TC-24; payment decision record is ADR-003. | One order to multiple immutable attempts; append/dedupe provider events; verify authenticity; apply state changes once; reconcile ambiguous ordering with provider or manual review. | Business + finance + technical lead are named for provider decisions in PRD; individual approvers unassigned. | Requirement confirmed; provider-specific design unresolved. | Coordinate ADR-003: provider, stable event key, signature verification, ordering/retry guarantees, attempt/order states, COD, refunds, duplicate-capture remedy, and reconciliation evidence. |
| How are money, currency, discounts, and rounding represented? | Stored amounts must reconcile across item totals, discounts, taxes, delivery fees, and provider transactions. | PRD §§2/3; PR-05, PR-08, PR-12, PR-14; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-06, TC-08, TC-09, TC-10, TC-11, TC-12, TC-18, TC-24. | Exact integer or fixed-precision arithmetic; no floating point; persist explicit currency and order-time values. Source identifies RWF but not precision or rounding. | Finance for tax/invoice; business owner for discount/commercial rules; engineering lead for representation. | Currency requirement confirmed; policies unresolved. | Approve RWF-only vs currency extensibility, integer vs decimal precision, discount stacking/allocation/residual rules, compare-at semantics, tax/discount interaction, and rounding stages/method. |
| What delivery zones, fees, tax, invoices, and EBM rules apply? | Checkout totals and order snapshots must agree with approved business/finance rules. | PR-05, PR-11, PR-14; Technical Plan §§2/6; QA TC-06, TC-17, TC-18. | Snapshot selected delivery rule/fee and approved tax treatment on the order; do not invent fee or tax rates or EBM behavior. | Operations owns delivery zones/fees; Finance owns VAT/invoice/EBM decisions (PRD §7). | Requirements confirmed; business rules unresolved. | Approve zones, fee matrix/free threshold/partner, tax-inclusive/exclusive policy, invoice fields, EBM requirement and verified integration path. |
| What user/staff authorization and audit model is needed? | Protect customer records and enforce least privilege without building unnecessary permission administration. | PRD §4; PR-06, PR-10, PR-13, PR-15; Technical Plan §6; QA TC-16, TC-20. | Enforce authorization server-side. Proposed MVP uses constrained staff roles/permissions for documented capabilities; requirements do not require runtime-configurable RBAC. Audit important privileged changes. | Engineering lead owns implementation responsibility per Technical Plan RACI; security/permission approver unassigned. | Least privilege and named roles confirmed; exact permission matrix unresolved. | Approve staff permissions, management/reporting scope, account/session design, audit event scope/access, and privacy/retention rules. |
| What constraints, FKs, indexes, and transactional boundaries are required? | Protect referential integrity, historical records, uniqueness, and checkout atomicity while keeping queries practical. | Technical Plan §§2/6; PR-02, PR-05, PR-08, PR-09, PR-12; ADR-001; QA TC-13, TC-14. | Review unique SKU/order/provider-event keys, positive quantities, explicit FK deletion rules, archive referenced catalogue rows, snapshot order data, and transact order+reservation together. Index actual access paths. | Technical Product Lead accountable / engineering lead responsible for architecture per Technical Plan RACI. | Proposed; approval needed. | Approve keys, nullability, checks, deletion/retention behavior, indexes against expected queries, and transaction boundaries. Review ADR-002. |
| What is required before models and Alembic revisions begin? | ADR-001 requires ADR-002 approval first; migration mistakes can affect production data and external payment/inventory effects. | ADR-001 migration paragraph; Technical Plan §§5/8/9; QA TC-23; ADR-002 implementation gate. | Record ADR-002 decision status, scope, named approvers/date and any conditions; verify RE-002/RE-003 linked evidence without taking over those tasks. Begin independent design/review work in parallel; gate only schema choices that depend on missing decisions. | Technical Product Lead accountable and engineering lead responsible for architecture per RACI; RE-002/RE-003 owners are teammates (names unavailable here). | Approval/evidence missing. | Before implementation: obtain required RE-002 taxonomy evidence and RE-003 dependency/completion evidence; resolve or explicitly defer schema-affecting decisions with approvers; approve ADR-002; then review first Alembic upgrade, constraints/indexes, seed data, clean/staging upgrade, downgrade/forward-fix limits, backup/restore rehearsal, and release sequencing. |

## Starting gate

Guest and registered-account checkout are both required. Generic entity design, independent requirement review, and decisions not dependent on teammate deliverables can proceed in parallel. Do not create commerce models or migrations until ADR-002's required design decisions and approvals are recorded, and verify RE-002/RE-003 dependency evidence through their owners rather than duplicating their work. Keep ADR-002 status **Proposed** until that approval evidence exists.
