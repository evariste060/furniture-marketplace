Delivery information

Last updated 20 Oct 2026 · Soma mu Kinyarwanda

We deliver to every district in Rwanda. You see the exact delivery fee at checkout before you place your order.

Delivery zones and fees

On delivery day

  • You get an SMS with the rider’s name and phone when your order leaves our store.
  • Give the rider the 4-digit delivery code from your SMS. Don’t share it before the parcel is in your hands.
  • Check the box is sealed before you sign. Cash on delivery is available in Kigali.
Address outside our zones? We’ll call to arrange delivery, or you can choose store pickup. Contact us
